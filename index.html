Mini-sessions & attendance

Explore. Try something. Ask a question. Learn together.

Meetings are in person, 2:00–3:00 p.m. A 15-minute mini-session opens each meeting. Let us know which dates you plan to attend, and if you'd like to lead a mini-session, add yourself as a presenter on any slot.

Mini-sessions don't need to be formal. Share something you tried, demo a workflow, walk through an assignment, show a prompt that worked (or didn't), or raise an AI question for the group. Presenters are encouraged to give the same session on both dates of a Friday/Tuesday pair, but one date is fine. If you can't come in person but would record a video of your session, that works too.

Type your name once, tick the dates you plan to attend, then press Submit my sign-up at the bottom. Entries are saved to a shared list that everyone sees.

Friday / Tuesday meeting pairs

Each pair repeats the same mini-session on a Friday and the following Tuesday. Tick either or both days, then submit at the bottom of the page.

Unpaired dates

Open for suggestions. If you'd like to share something on one of these dates, add yourself as a presenter.
